# Adopters

This project has no telemetry and never will, so **self-reporting is the only way we know anyone is using it.** One row tells contributors who they are building for.

## What goes in a row

- **Your organization's name, or just a sector.** "A European food distributor, ~2,000 staff" is a complete entry, and often the only one legal will approve.
- **Which skills you run.**
- **Nothing else.** Never record what a skill told you — the decision, the option chosen, application names, costs, or supplier terms. **An entry says that you use a method, never what it concluded.**

## How to add yourself

Open a pull request adding one row, or [an issue](../../issues) titled `Adopter: <name>` and a maintainer will add it.

By adding a row you confirm you have the authority to name your organization. We remove any entry on request from anyone at the organization named, no questions asked.

Using this as an individual rather than for an organization? [TESTIMONIALS.md](TESTIMONIALS.md) is the right file.
