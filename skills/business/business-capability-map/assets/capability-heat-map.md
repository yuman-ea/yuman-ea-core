> **The weighted score orders attention. It is not the rating.** A capability scoring 5 on
> importance and 1 on process maturity averages to "adequate" and is a hotspot. The status
> comes from the pattern; the total is a sorting aid.

## Statuses used

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `hotspot` | Matters a lot, performs badly on at least one dimension |
| `fragile` | Adequate today, but resting on one person or one unsupported system |
| `overinvested` | Commodity work carrying differentiating-level cost or attention |
| `adequate` | Nothing here needs a decision |
| `strength` | Differentiating and performing — protect it, do not optimise it |
| `insufficient_evidence` | Could not be rated |
