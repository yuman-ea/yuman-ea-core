## How to use this, and how not to

**Use it to:** frame where investment is argued, give two domains a shared vocabulary, and
find work being done twice.

**Do not use it to:** propose a structure, justify a headcount decision, or compare against
another organization's model that was drawn to different boundaries at a different depth.

**And do not present the heat map without this document.** The colours are the part people
remember and the derivation is the part that makes them defensible.
