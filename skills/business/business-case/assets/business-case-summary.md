## Deliberately not decided here

- **Whether to fund this.** This makes the argument; someone accountable decides.
- Whether to build or buy — that runs first, and is a different method.
- Which supplier or product.
- **Whether this is the best use of the money.** Ranking against other investments competing
  for the same budget is a portfolio decision, and a strong case for one thing says nothing
  about the others.
- The delivery sequence.
