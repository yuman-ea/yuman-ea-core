## What this register does not claim

- **That any system should be replaced.** A system appearing against a pain point is a
  finding. Whether it should be replaced rests on cost, contract, and estate evidence this
  method never sees, and belongs to `application-rationalization`.
- **That any of these is worth fixing.** This says what the pain costs the customer. What it
  costs to fix, and whether that pays, is a `business-case` question.
- **That the list is complete.** Where pain was inferred internally, it is a list of what
  the organization already knew, reorganised.
