## What would change this

{Sensitivity results. Lead with the opposite-of-the-stated-preference test: whether the
recommendation survives being designed the other way. It is the question this design will be
challenged on regardless of whether it appears here.}

## Deliberately not decided here

- **What the organization does.** That is the capability model, which this consumes.
- How mature the current capabilities are.
- **Any decision about specific people.** This names roles.
- The order of changes against delivery capacity — that is roadmap sequencing.
- Whether to build, buy, or partner for any capability the model needs.
- Whether the change pays for itself.
