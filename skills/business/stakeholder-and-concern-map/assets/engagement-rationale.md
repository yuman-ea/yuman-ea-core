## Deliberately not decided here

- **Resolving any conflict between stakeholders.** This records them; resolution is a
  decision belonging to the named person in the matrix.
- Producing the views themselves. This says which are needed and what each must answer.
- Which domain owns a question — that is the orchestrator's.
- **Whether a stated concern is reasonable.** A concern held by someone who can stop the
  work is material whether or not it is well founded.
