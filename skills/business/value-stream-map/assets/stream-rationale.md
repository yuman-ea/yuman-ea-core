## Deliberately not decided here

- Which systems to replace. Friction at a system is a finding, not a disposition.
- How the organization should be arranged. This shows where work sticks, not who should own it.
- The detailed design of any single process, with its tasks and controls.
- The customer's experience of the stream, and which systems they touch.
- Any redesign. This is the as-is shape and, where requested, a to-be shape — not a plan.
