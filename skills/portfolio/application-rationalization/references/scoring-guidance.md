# Scoring guidance

Use the common 1–5 favourability scale from `skill.yaml`. Score the **evidence**, not the reputation of the application or the seniority of its sponsor.

## Business value

- **5** — critical to material revenue, customer/employee outcomes, regulated operations, or a strategic differentiator; no practical substitute today.
- **4** — important and widely used; disruption would cause a clear business impact.
- **3** — useful but substitutable, limited in scope, or mixed evidence of continuing value.
- **2** — declining or narrow use with modest business impact and available workarounds.
- **1** — business need is gone, fully covered elsewhere, or owner confirms no continuing value.

## Technical fitness

- **5** — supported, maintainable, reliable, performant, observable, and operable for the horizon.
- **4** — healthy with manageable known issues.
- **3** — mixed: serviceable but with technical debt or a lifecycle concern that is not yet urgent.
- **2** — material supportability, maintainability, reliability, or performance weakness.
- **1** — unsupported or structurally unfit for continued operation without significant intervention.

## Risk posture

- **5** — current controls and obligations are met with no material unresolved exposure.
- **4** — minor issues with accepted and funded remediation.
- **3** — mixed evidence, temporary exceptions, or moderate unresolved risk.
- **2** — material security, compliance, resilience, or data-handling exposure.
- **1** — unacceptable unresolved exposure or confirmed non-compliance that demands action.

## Strategic fit

- **5** — directly enables the stated target operating model or strategic capability for the horizon.
- **4** — clearly aligned and likely to remain important.
- **3** — neutral or uncertain strategic relevance.
- **2** — directionally inconsistent with the target portfolio.
- **1** — explicitly outside the target state or scheduled to be replaced by strategy.

## Cost efficiency

Use comparable costs only. Where costs are not comparable, omit this criterion rather than inventing precision.

- **5** — strong value for cost relative to comparable applications serving similar scale or criticality.
- **4** — reasonable cost with no material inefficiency identified.
- **3** — mixed or average cost efficiency.
- **2** — materially expensive for the value and usage delivered.
- **1** — clearly disproportionate cost with little justified value.

## Portfolio uniqueness

- **5** — distinct capability with no meaningful overlap.
- **4** — mostly distinct; overlap is limited and justified.
- **3** — partial overlap but no validated consolidation path.
- **2** — substantial validated duplication with another application.
- **1** — near-complete duplication with a stronger target already covering the need.

## Change feasibility

- **5** — change is low dependency, contractually unconstrained, owner-supported, and feasible within the horizon.
- **4** — manageable dependencies and transition effort.
- **3** — meaningful dependencies or sequencing needs but feasible with planning.
- **2** — high transition complexity, contractual constraints, or organizational capacity limits.
- **1** — change is currently blocked by a hard constraint, critical dependency, or unacceptable continuity risk.

## Evidence discipline

Every score cites the evidence ID or source slice. A score without a source is an opinion with arithmetic attached.
